How It Works

Costume Contest Process

1

Register & Opt In

When you register, indicate you'd like to participate in the costume contest. You can also sign up at check-in on the day of the event.

2

Walk the 5K in Style

Show off your costume on the route! Interact with sculptures, get photos taken, and enjoy being a living work of art as you walk through Michigan City.

3

Judging at 1:10 PM

After returning to Millennium Plaza, official judging begins at 1:10 PM. All costume contest participants should be in the designated area by this time.

Please ensure your costume is safe for walking outdoors. Costumes should not block your vision, create tripping hazards, or obstruct other participants. Comfortable footwear is strongly recommended — you'll be walking 5 kilometers!

Silent Auction & Community Choice

The Leopold Bench Project

19 one-of-a-kind works of art, painted by local artists, ready to become a permanent piece of your home or community.

About the Project

Art You Can Sit On

The Michigan City Public Art Committee (MAC) commissioned local artists to transform Leopold benches — durable, beautiful outdoor benches — into unique works of public art placed throughout Michigan City.

🎨

Local Artists, One-of-a-Kind Creations

Each of the 19 Leopold benches was entrusted to a local Michigan City artist who brought their own unique vision and creativity to the piece. No two benches are alike — these are truly singular works of art that are also functional, durable outdoor furniture. These benches are placed throughout Michigan City as part of our public art collection.

🏆

Silent Auction — Own a Piece of Michigan City Art History

At the Walking Art 5K, all 19 painted Leopold benches will be available through a silent auction. Each bench is a collectible, investment-grade piece of local art history. Bidding opens when the festival begins and closes at approximately 12:30 PM when participants return from the walk. Winners announced from the main stage at 1:45 PM.

⭐

Community Choice Award

Every festival attendee can vote for their favorite bench artwork. The artist behind the bench receiving the most community votes will receive the prestigious Community Choice Award — a special recognition from MAC and the Michigan City community for exceptional artistic achievement. The winning artist receives the award at the 1:45 PM announcement.

How It Works

Auction & Voting Guide

🪑 Silent Auction

  • All 19 benches displayed at the festival
  • Each bench has a bid sheet with starting bid
  • Write your bid — highest bid wins
  • Bidding closes at ~12:30 PM
  • Winners announced at 1:45 PM from the stage
  • Arrangements made for bench pickup/delivery
  • Proceeds support MAC public art programs

⭐ Community Choice Vote

  • View all 19 benches on display
  • Pick up a voting card at check-in or MAC info booth
  • Vote for your favorite bench artwork
  • Drop your vote in the ballot box
  • Votes tallied after auction closes
  • Winning artist announced at 1:45 PM
  • Artist receives MAC Community Choice Award

Free App

Discover Art with Otocast

The Otocast app transforms your walk into an immersive audio art tour — hear the artists speak in their own words, collect digital badges, and explore Michigan City's entire public art collection.

What is Otocast?

Art That Speaks to You

Otocast is a free mobile app that creates location-based audio art tours. As you approach a sculpture or mural, the app automatically begins playing a pre-recorded message from the artist themselves — explaining the significance of their work, their inspiration, and their creative process — right in their own voice.

🗺️

Interactive Art Map

See all of Michigan City's public artworks on an interactive map — works on the 5K route AND throughout the greater Michigan City area, all in one place.

🎙️

Hear the Artists' Voices

Within just 15 feet of a sculpture, the app automatically begins playing a pre-recorded message from the artist. Hear them describe the meaning and story behind their work — in their own words.

🏅

Collect Digital Badges

Visit artworks and earn digital achievement badges! Build your collection, explore more of the city, and show off your art adventures on social media.

🎟️

Enter the Raffle

Using the Otocast app during the Walking Art 5K gives you access to the raffle — an exciting extra bonus for participants who explore the route digitally!

🌆

Beyond the 5K Route

The Otocast collection covers not just the 5K route, but murals and sculptures scattered throughout the entire greater Michigan City area. Keep exploring long after the event!

📍

Location-Aware

The app uses your phone's GPS to detect when you're near a work of art. No searching required — just walk and the art experience comes to you automatically.

Getting Started

How to Use Otocast

1

Download the Free App

Search for "Otocast" in the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android). The app is completely free to download and use.

2

Search for the MAC Michigan City Collection

Once in the app, search for the Michigan City Public Art Committee (MAC) collection to access all sculptures and murals in our collection.

3

Enable Location Services

Allow the app to access your location so it can automatically trigger audio tours and badge awards as you approach artworks within 15 feet.

4

Walk, Listen & Collect!

Explore Michigan City, listen to artists in their own words, earn badges, enter the raffle, and enjoy our amazing public art scene — today and every day after!

💡 Pro Tip: Download Before the Event!

We recommend downloading the Otocast app and setting up your profile before October 10th. That way you're ready to start collecting badges and hearing artist stories from the very first sculpture on the route!

Important Requirements

What to Know

⛺

Bring Your Own Tent

All vendors must provide their own shelter, tables, chairs, and display equipment. Everything must fit within your designated space. No staking allowed — paved surface.

⚖️

Canopy Weights Required

This is an outdoor lakefront event. All canopies must have a minimum of 25 pounds of weight on each leg. Wind safety is mandatory — no exceptions.

🚗

Vehicle Access

Vehicles allowed in the event area only during load-in (before 9:30 AM) and load-out (after 2:00 PM). Plan your setup accordingly.

⚡

No Power or Water

Power and water are NOT provided at vendor spaces. Please plan your display accordingly.

📋

Contract & Insurance

Vendors must sign a contract including an Indemnification & Hold Harmless statement. Certificate of Insurance required — City of Michigan City named as Additional Insured.

🕐

Set-Up & Tear-Down

Set-up window: 7:30–9:30 AM. All vendors must be ready by 9:30 AM. Tear-down begins at 2:00 PM after the festival closes.
